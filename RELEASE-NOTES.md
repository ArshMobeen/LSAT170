Angel's 170 is a complete desktop app for Angel Thukral.

- **Apple Silicon MacBook:** download the `arm64.dmg` file.
- **Intel MacBook:** download the `x64.dmg` file.
- Open the DMG, drag Angels 170 into Applications, then open it from Applications.
- Requires **macOS 13 Ventura or newer**. No developer tools are needed.

This personal build is ad-hoc signed, not Apple-notarized. On first launch, macOS may require **System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway** after you try opening the app. See [Apple's instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445).

Includes all four Reset Room modes, fullscreen focus, daily journals and study logs, motivation library, personal letters from Arsh, vision board, backup export/restore, and the custom navy-and-silver app icon. Saved records persist locally after closing; unfinished focus sessions reopen paused.

Download `INSTALL-ON-MAC.txt` for instructions. `SHA256SUMS.txt` contains the installer checksums.
