ANGEL'S 170
Made for Angel, with love from Arsh.

INSTALL
1. In Apple menu > About This Mac, look for Chip or Processor.
   Apple M1/M2/M3/M4/M5 or newer: use the arm64 DMG.
   Intel: use the x64 DMG.
2. Open the DMG and drag Angels 170 into Applications.
3. Eject the DMG. Open Angels 170 from Applications.

Requires macOS 13 Ventura or newer. No Node.js, coding tools or account needed.

FIRST OPEN
This personal build is locally signed, but not Apple-notarized.
If macOS says the developer cannot be verified, first try opening the app.
Then go to System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway, and confirm Open.
This approval is specific to this app. Do not turn off Mac security protection.
Apple's guide: https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445

YOUR DATA
Journals, logged hours, scores, photos, notes and settings save automatically on
this Mac. An unfinished focus timer reopens paused. Use Finish & save to log it.
Settings & backup lets you export a backup or restore one from another device.
The Reset Room's drawings and disposable vent writing are temporary unless saved.

Use the app from Applications, not from inside the mounted DMG. Your saved data
lives separately in your Mac's user profile and stays when the app closes.
